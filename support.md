# Support

Questions, problems or ideas: write to [ayers177@gmail.com](mailto:ayers177@gmail.com). Say which device (Apple TV, iPhone or iPad), which version of Boxset (Settings, at the foot), and what happened.

## Signing in

Boxset signs in with your Plex account: choose **Sign in with Plex**, then enter the code shown at **plex.tv/link** on a phone or a computer. To look round first, **Try the demo library** opens an invented library; Settings > **Leave the demo** returns to the first screen.

## Nothing shows on Home

Home says **Can't reach** your server when the computer it runs on is asleep, switched off, or not on the same network. Wake it, then **Try again**. Away from home, your server needs Plex's remote access turned on.

## Who's in the scene

It needs the cast's photos from your Plex server; it does best with films and shows whose cast Plex lists with photos. It's asked about on the first pause; Settings > **Who is in the scene** has the switch, an optional TMDB key for more photos of each actor, and **Forget what it has learned**.

## Subtitles and audio

On Apple TV, swipe down (or hold the centre of the remote) for chapters, audio, subtitles and their timing. On a channel, Up and Down change channel: hold the centre instead.

## Requests

Settings > **Requests** takes your own Overseerr or Seerr server's address, as it appears in your browser. If the address shows a sign-in page first (such as Cloudflare Access), Boxset can't get through it: use an address that opens the request server directly.

## Channels

The Channels tab makes channels from your library and keeps them in your iCloud, so they appear on your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account.
