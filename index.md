# A player for your own Plex library

<p class="lead">Boxset plays your films, shows and music from your own Plex Media Server, on Apple TV, iPhone and iPad. Pause, and see who's in the scene.</p>

## Who's in the scene

Pause a film or an episode and Boxset shows which of the cast are in the picture, by matching the faces it sees to the cast's photos from your server. It happens on your device, and it asks first. Choose an actor for their page: the moments they appear in what you're watching, everything of theirs in your library, and, if you run Overseerr or Seerr, everything else they've been in, ready to request.

## Channels

Turn your library into channels that are always on: a genre at random, a series in order that keeps your place, films with music videos between them, a different lineup by time of day. The guide shows what's on now and next.

## Plays what you have

Direct play of most files, HDR10 and Dolby Vision on Apple TV, surround sound, chapters, subtitles with timing and size, audio sync, skipping intros and credits, the next episode on its own. On iPhone and iPad: downloads for offline viewing, Picture in Picture and AirPlay.

## What you need

A Plex account and a Plex Media Server with your own media. Try it first without one: **Try the demo library** on the first screen opens an invented library.
